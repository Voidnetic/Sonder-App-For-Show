Where the name comes from
sonder
noun

The realisation that every passer-by has a life as vivid and complex as your own.

From The Dictionary of Obscure Sorrows by John Koenig

Friends at the same event each live their own version of it. Our app is named after that feeling: it gathers every version of a shared moment in one place.

to a shared reveal

2

3

Chat in the Hangout, see who's here and who's typing while the timer runs.

4

Reveal

Speaker 1. Firebase Security Rules

Firestore offline persistence
An offline upload queue: photos wait privately on the phone and upload later

Speaker 2. "Our design document planned Firebase. While building, we moved to our own server so we fully control the lock: the server simply never gives out a photo link before the unlock time." Walk the rows: the lock requirement (FR7) is met on the server; offline reliability became an upload queue; AI features and the early-unlock vote moved to the roadmap so the core loop could be finished and tested; and we added features users asked for while testing.
